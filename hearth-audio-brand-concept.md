# Hearth Audio: Brand Concept

**Direction:** The Craft Studio, with a touch of The Listening Room
*Updated 17 Oct 2026: the feel changed from warm and cozy to sleek and premium, like a luxury audio showroom.*

## Mission
To let anyone in Kenya hear premium speakers in their own home, one month at a time, so they can find the sound they love before they ever spend a shilling on owning it.

## Primary audience
People like Christopher: 25 to 40 year old professionals who love music, have a home worth listening in, and want to explore different speaker tunings without paying around KES 400k for each one.

## Positioning against competitors
- **Shops and demo rooms:** They give you 10 minutes in a room that isn't yours. Hearth Audio gives you a month in your own living room.
- **YouTube and online reviews:** They give you someone else's ears through a phone speaker. Hearth Audio gives you your own ears, plus honest notes from real renters.
- **Buying outright:** It locks you into one sound and a big bill. Hearth Audio lets you change your mind every month.

We are the precise, home-based way to find your sound, with the care of a specialist.

## Brand pillars
1. **Listen at home.** Real judgement happens in your own space, not a showroom.
2. **Precise, not showy.** We care about tuning, build, and detail, and we state them exactly.
3. **Shared ears.** Every listening note makes the next person's choice better.

## Voice and tone
Calm, exact, and quietly confident. Short sentences, specific facts, and no hype. We sound like a knowledgeable specialist who respects your time.

**Five words we always use:** listen, tuning, precise, considered, quiet

**Five words we never use:** cheap, hack, deal, bargain, gear-head

## Visual feel
Hearth Audio looks like a luxury audio showroom at night. There is a lot of empty space, and each page shows one speaker at a time. Colours are near-black graphite and ivory, with a single restrained champagne-gold accent. Lines are thin, alignment is exact, and ornament is kept to a minimum. Photography shows one product against a plain background in soft, controlled light. The hearth arch icon appears in graphite and ivory.

## Open question
The fonts (Young Serif and Hanken Grotesk) and the Lamplight palette were chosen for the earlier warm direction. They are under review for the sleek and premium feel.
